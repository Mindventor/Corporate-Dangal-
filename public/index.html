How to fight

On a phoneThe stick is bottom-left: push to move, up to jump, down to duck, flick twice to run, hold away to block. Buttons are on the right; SPECIAL does your company's special in one tap.
A D or ← →Move. Double-tap toward to run, away to backstep. Hold away to block; add S to block sweeps.
W / SJump (tap for a short hop) / duck under jabs and thrown objects.
J J JJab, cross, hook. Also JJK (ends in a launcher), JKK and KKJ (floor bounce).
← →+JYour company's special: tap away, then toward + punch. S+I or U does it too. Works mid-string.
TTaunt. You stand there being rude for a second; finish it and you gain Hype, get hit and you do not. Specials need 2.5 seconds between uses.
Super clashWhen they start a super and your Hype is full, fire yours during the flash. Both of you then race through six keys in order; first to finish lands their super. Time out and the first one wins.
Guard barThe thin bar under your health. Blocking drains it; specials and supers drain it fast. Empty means guard break. Block at the last moment for a free perfect block.
S+JUppercut. Launches them so you can keep hitting in the air.
S+KSweep. Must be blocked ducking.
→+J / →+KShoulder charge / throw your company's signature object.
In the air J / KOverhead punch / dive kick. Must be blocked standing.
IGrab and slam. Beats blocking. Hold away to throw them behind you.
L or SpaceSignature super when the Hype meter is full. Works mid-combo.
Esc / NPause / mute. Matches are best of three quarters.

Two players: P1 uses WASD with F punch, G kick, H grab, R super. P2 uses the arrow keys with J K I L.

How ranking works

Climbing the ladder

Online matches against other people earn rank points (RP): one-on-one rooms and rumbles both count. CPU fighters do not. Set your name on the Play online screen.

Streak: from your third win in a row, each win is worth 5 more. Probation: Interns (under 100 RP) never lose points. Walking out mid-match counts as finishing last, and RP never drops below zero.
